# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SparkCall aims to be a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project. Participants must help keep discussions and contributions professional and safe, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving and receiving feedback with care.
- Focusing on ideas, evidence, and user needs rather than people.
- Respecting different experiences, viewpoints, and boundaries.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm where possible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private or sensitive information. Do not share credentials, call recordings, call metadata, or other private communications in public project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions and may limit participation when behavior conflicts with this standard. They will use their judgment to protect participants and the project, with a preference for clear, proportionate, and consistent action.

Report conduct concerns privately through a private GitHub reporting channel only when one is enabled for this repository, or through a private contact channel explicitly published by a maintainer. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer for this project is **BOOMDEE**.

If no private channel is published, request one without naming people, describing the incident, or attaching sensitive evidence in the public request.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
